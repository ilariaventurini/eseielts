# sentence linkers

## 🌓 contrast
* **although**: sebbene/nonostante (introduce una subordinata)
* **by/in comparison**: in confronto/a paragone
* **conversely**: al contrario/viceversa
* **despite/in spite of**: nonostante/malgrado
* **however**: tuttavia/però
* **in contrast**: in contrasto/al contrario
* **instead**: invece
* **nevertheless/nonetheless**: ciò nonostante/tuttavia
* **on the contrary**: al contrario (per smentire quanto detto prima)
* **on the other hand**: d'altra parte/d'altro canto
* **whereas/while**: mentre/laddove
* **in (complete) contrast to**: in (netto) contrasto con
* **unlike**: a differenza di/diversamente da
* **compared to**: rispetto a/paragonato a
* **a major difference**: una differenza importante
* **the biggest/main/clearest difference**: la differenza principale/più chiara

## 🤝 similarity
* **in the same way**: allo stesso modo
* **likewise**: allo stesso modo/parimenti
* **similarly**: similmente/in modo simile
* **similar to**: simile a
* **have things in common**: avere cose in comune

## 📈 result
* **accordingly**: di conseguenza/in conformità
* **as a consequence/as a result**: come conseguenza/risultato
* **hence/therefore/thus**: quindi/perciò/pertanto/in tal modo

## 🔢 sequence
* **at first glance**: a prima vista
* **first/firstly/first of all**: in primo luogo/per prima cosa
* **second/secondly**: in secondo luogo
* **third/thirdly**: in terzo luogo
* **besides/furthermore/in addition/additionally/moreover/what is more/on top of that…**: inoltre/oltre a ciò/in aggiunta
* **subsequently**: successivamente/in seguito
* **last/lastly**: in ultimo/infine
* **another point worth noting is…/another factor to consider is…****: va anche fatto notare che… (*another point worth noting is that consumers…*)

## 💡 examples
* **for example/for instance/such as**: per esempio

# verbs for describing data

* **analyze**: analizzare
* **compare/contrast**: confrontare/mettere a confronto

* **demonstrate/show/indicate**: dimostrare/mostrare/indicare
* **depict/portray/represent**: raffigurare/ritrattare/rappresentare
* **describe**: descrivere
* **display/present**: mostrare/visualizzare/presentare
* **evaluate**: valutare
* **highlight/illustrate**: evidenziare/illustrare
* **summarize**: riassumere

# data movements

## 🔼 increase
* **go up/grow/increase/rise**: salire/crescere/aumentare
* **double/triple**: raddoppiare/triplicare (*Profits doubled from year 1 to year 4*, *the amount of compost nearly tripled over the period shown*, *The profits in year 4 were double/twice the profits in year 1*, *2009 statistics show triple / three times the amount of recycling as 2006*)
* **twofold/threefold**: *There was a twofold increase from year 1 to year 4*, *the amount of compost increased nearly threefold over the period shown in the chart*
* **double/triple the…**: doppio/triplo rispetto a…
* **improve**: migliorare
* **skyrocket/soar**: schizzare alle stelle/impennarsi (*our website traffic will skyrocket tomorrow*, *make use of this trading mechanism to see your profits soar*)
* **surge**: ondata/forte aumento improvviso *the recent surge of data highlighted the need for updated technology*
* **upward trend**: tendenza al rialzo *if we average the data, it shows an upward trend in sales*
* **higher/bigger than**: più alto/grande di

## 🔽 decrease
* **decline/decrease/drop/fall/go down**: calare/diminuire/cadere/scendere
* **dip**: flettere (temporaneamente)
* **half**: *The number of people traveling by bus was half as much in 2005 when compared to 2010*
* **halve**: dimezzare
* **plummet/slump**: crollare/precipitare
* **reduce**: ridurre
* **downward trend/descending trend**: tendenza al ribasso
* **lower/smaller than**: più basso/piccolo di

## ↔️ no change/ups and downs
* **be unchanged**: rimanere invariato
* **level out/stabilize**: assestarsi/stabilizzarsi
* **plateau/reach a plateau**: raggiungere una fase di stasi
* **remain stable/constant/steady**: rimanere stabile
* **fluctuate**: fluttuare
* **change intermittently/sporadically**: cambiare a intermittenza
* **rise and fall**: alti e bassi
* **as high as/as much as**: tanto quanto/alto quanto

## agreeing and disagreeing

* **i strongly agree with…** (eg, i strongly agree with the notion that education is crucial)
* **i completely disagree with…** (eg, i completely disagree with the idea that homework is unnecessary)
* **i am of the same opinion that…** (eg, i am of the same opinion that technology has improved our lives)
* **while i agree with some aspects…** (eg, while i agree with some aspects of the argument, i disagree with its overall conclusion)
* **i concur with the viewpoint that…** (eg, i concur with the viewpoint that social media has a negative impact on mental health)
* **i cannot accept the argument that…**
* **i subscribe to the view that…**
* **it is hard to agree with the notion that…**
* **i am in complete agreement with…**
* **i take issue with the idea that…**

---

## causes and effects

* **…due to…** (eg, the increase in air pollution is due to the growing number of cars)
* **as a result of…** (eg, the company's profits decreased as a result of the economic crisis)
* **one of the main reasons for… is…** (eg, one of the main reasons for the decline in tourism is the ongoing pandemic)
* **…caused by…** (eg, the decline in bee populations is caused by the use of pesticides)
* **the consequences of… are…** (eg, the consequences of climate change are becoming increasingly apparent)
* **consequently,…**
* **therefore,…**
* **this has led to…**
* **as a consequence of this,…**
* **this results in…**

---

## contrasting and comparing

* **in the same way that…** (eg, in the same way that regular exercise is beneficial for physical health, reading is beneficial for mental health)
* **in a similar vein,…** (eg, many people enjoy listening to music to relax. in a similar vein, reading can also be a great way to unwind)
* **this can be explained by the fact that…** (eg, the traffic on the highway was heavy because of an accident. this can be explained by the fact tha accidents cause delays and congestion)
* **by contrast,…**
* **on the contrary,…**
* **in comparison with…**
* **conversely,…**
* **unlike [x], [y] tends to…**
* **while some people prefer… others choose…**
* **similarly,…**

---

## clarifying and paraphrasing

* **in other words,…** (eg, the research shows that many people who quit smoking gain weight. in other words, weight gain is a common side effect o quitting smoking.)
* **to put it another way,…** (eg, some people enjoy the hustle and bustle of city life. to put it another way, they thrive in busy surroundings)
* **that is to say,…**
* **to clarify this point,…**
* **what this means is that…**

---

## emphasizing importance

* **vitally important…** (eg, reducing carbon emissions is vitally important for preventing the worst impacts of climate change)
* **it is essential that…** (eg, it is essential that governments take action to address climate change and reduce carbon emissions)
* **this is of paramount importance because…** (eg, this is of paramount importance because accurate data collection is crucial for making informe decisions in the field of medicine.)
* **it is worth noting that…** (eg, it is worth noting that many successful entrepreneurs started their businesses with very little capital)
* **it is crucial to understand that…** (eg, it is crucial to understand that good time management skills are essential for academic success)
* **of significant importance is…**
* **it should be emphasized that…**
* **central to this issue is…**

---

## giving reasons

* **the primary factor behind… is…** (eg, the primary factor behind the rise in global temperatures is the increasing levels of greenhouse gas emissions)
* **the reason for this is that…** (eg, the reason for this is that air pollution has a detrimental effect on human health)
* **the underlying reason for…** (eg, the underlying reason for the rise in crime rates is the lack of economic opportunities in the area)
* **…on account of…** (eg, the company's profits have declined on account of the increase in production costs)
* **this is largely due to…**
* **this can be attributed to…**
* **owing to the fact that…**

---

## supporting arguments and evidence

* **research has shown that…** (eg, research has shown that regular exercise can improve mental health and reduce the risk of depression)
* **as evidence shows,…** (eg, as evidence shows, increasing access to healthcare can lead to improved health outcomes and reduced healthcare costs)
* **for instance,…**
* **to illustrate this point,…**
* **a clear example of this is…**
* **as can be seen in recent studies,…**
* **case in point,…**

---

## highlighting key points

* **it is essential to note that…** (eg, it is essential to note that the findings of the study are based on a limited sample size)
* **a key point to consider when… is…** (eg, a key point to consider when discussing the impact of social media on mental health is the potential fo cyberbullying.)
* **significantly,…**
* **most notably,…**
* **an important aspect to highlight is…**

---

## providing alternatives

* **on the other hand,…** (eg, the new policy may create job opportunities, but on the other hand, it may also increase inflation)
* **another option to consider is…** (eg, the proposed solution may not be the most effective. another option to consider is investing in research an development.)
* **alternatively,…**
* **as an alternative approach,…**
* **an alternative solution would be to…**

---

## acknowledging other viewpoints

* **it is true that…** (eg, it is true that technology has revolutionized the way we live and work)
* **one counterargument is that…** (eg, one counterargument is that stricter gun control laws would infringe upon individuals' rights)
* **admittedly,…**
* **while it can be argued that…**
* **granted, some people believe that…**
* **opponents of this view argue that…**

---

## certainty and probability (hedging)

* **it is highly likely that…** (eg, it is highly likely that wind and solar power will become increasingly important)
* **it is possible that…** (eg, it is possible that artificial intelligence could transform the job market)
* **it is certain that…** (eg, it is certain that climate change is a pressing issue that requires immediate action)
* **it is doubtful that…** (eg, it is doubtful that a single solution can fix the complex issue of global poverty)
* **it is highly probable that…** (eg, it is highly probable that remote working options will expand)
* **there is a strong possibility that…**
* **it appears that…/it seems that…**
