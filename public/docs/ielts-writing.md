# IELTS writing

2 testi in 60 minuti. Si può scegliere come gestire il tempo e l’ordine in cui affrontare le prove.

1. task 1 - testo descrittivo
   - minimo 150 parole
   - vale un ⅓ del voto
   - ottimo da fare in 20 min
   - vengono mostrati uno o due grafici che vanno descritti in maniera oggettiva. no mettere supposizioni, opinioni personali
   - voto da 0 a 9
   - 3 paragrafi

2. task 2 - testo argomentativo
   - minimo 250 parole
   - vale ⅔ del voto
   - ottimo da fare in 40 min
   - generalmente viene considerato più difficile ma per gli italiani risulta più facile perchè c’è anche una parte creativa, personale
   - voto da 0 a 9
   - 4 paragrafi

Note:
- non usare _we_ o _you_, usare _it is possible to see_
- usare 3.5 e non 3,5 (cioè usare il punto e non la virgola)
- non scrivere i numeri in cifre
- non usare forme abbreviate tipo _it’s_, _doesn’t_ ma _it is_, _does not_
- separare bene i paragrafi con una riga vuota.

## Task 1 - Describing data & trends
Grafici possibili:
- barchart
- linechart
- pie chart (di solito, siccome c’è poco da dire, ce ne sono due o tre insieme così si possono descrivere e confrontare)
- table
- mappe (raro)
- process diagram (raro): bisogna descrivere un processo, molto tecnico e lineare
- o combinazioni tra loro

Strutturare il testo in 3 paragrafi:
1. main feature
2. main body 1
3. main body 2

### Paragrafo 1 - Main feature
identificare una feature che salta subito all’occhio, se ce ne sono più di una, concentrarsi su una sola.
si compone di introduction + overview:
- **introduction**: begin the text with one or two sentences that state what the IELTS writing task 1 shows. To do this, paraphrase the task sentence, using synonyms or changing the form of the words (eg: employment, employed). do not copy-paste. examples:
  - _The proportion of the population aged 50 and above in three different states in the USA between 1920 and 2020 is shown in the graphs below._
  → _The line graphs compare the percentage of individuals aged 50 and above, as measured over a century in three U.S. states._
  - _The charts below show the reasons why people travel to work by bicycle or by car._
  → _These graphs display why commuters arrive at their place of work on their bike or in their car._
  - _The table shows the proportions of pupils attending four secondary school types between 2000 and 2009._
  → _The table illustrates the changes in students’ choices of secondary schools throughout a nine-year period starting from 2000._
- **overview**: the introduction must be followed by an overview, where the main trends of the data are presented. Start the Overview with _“Overall_”. Non specificare dati precisi. examples:
  - _Overall, the consumption of fish and chips declined over the period, whereas the amount of pizza and hamburgers that were eaten increased._

### Paragrafo 2 - Main body 1
Describe the main trends and key features already mentioned in the Overview, by providing more details. It is important to use sentence linkers, which are often followed by a comma.
Presentiamo con dati precisi specifici (date, numeri, cifre, percentuali) una parte del grafico, meglio concentrarsi sulla main feature

### Paragrafo 3 - Main body 2
Make comparisons where possible, identifying similarities and differences among data. It is important to use sentence linkers.
Non ci vuole nessuna conclusione (che di solito è personale).
Examples of comparisons:
- The USA produces large amounts of natural gas. _In contrast_, South Korea produces none.
European countries make great use of solar power. _On the other hand_, most Asian countries use this - method of power generation very little.
- The Middle East produces high levels of oil, _whereas/while_ Japan produces none.
- _Although_ the Middle East produced 100 tons of oil, Japan produced none.
- Solar power accounts for _far less_ of the total energy production _than_ gas or coal does.
- Hydropower is not _as_ efficient _as_ wind power.
- _Like_ Japan, South Korea does not produce any natural gas.
- The Middle East produces _twice as much_ oil as Europe.
- Western countries consume _three times more_ oil _than_ the Middle East.
- Russia consumes _slightly more_ oil _than_ Germany.
- The USA produced _the same_ amount of oil _as_ Saudi Arabia.

### Come si viene valutati?
Esistono 4 criteri di valutazione:
1. task achievement
   - faccio quello che mi viene chiesto di fare: essere obiettivi, no opinioni, considerazioni personali
   - bisogna mettere dati precisi, cioè quelli del grafico
   - bisogna rispettare la consegna che viene data insieme al grafico
2. struttura
   - testo coeso e scorrevole, no testo schematico. usare i sentence linkers
   - va benissimo creare frasi corte, con punteggiatura. però inizia le nuove frasi con i sentence linkers
   - importante la divisione in paragrafi (per esempio 3), inserire linea vuota tra un paragrafo e l’altro
3. grammatica
   - grammatical accuracy (precisione e correttezza): scrivere semplice semplice
   - grammatical range (importante per C1 e C2)
4. lessico
   - usare parole per la descrizione dei grafici (vedi glossario)

## Task 2 - Essay writing
250 words on a topic:
1. 5 min: planning
   - identificare argomento della traccia e i due elementi da argomentare
   - brainstorming dei due elementi
   - meglio poche cose spiegate bene che una lista poco approfondita
   - don’t rush writing
   - read the task carefully and figure out which type is it
   - generate ideas
   - structure the essay
2. 30 min: writing
   - structure
   - introduction (40-60 words)
   - body paragraph 1 (70-100 words)
   - body paragraph 2 (70-100 words)
   - conclusion (40-60 words)
3. 5 min: proof-read

Note:
- 250 words
- vale ⅔ del voto
- 4 paragrafi: introduction, main body 1, main body 2, conclusion
- ci danno un argomento e due elementi: sei d’accordo o meno, vantaggi e svantaggi, alcuni la pensano in un modo e altri in un altro, cause e possibili soluzioni
- use sample and boring ideas, you are not assessed on how interesting your essay is
- make sure your opinion is clear throughout the whole essay
- write well-developed ideas
- don’t overgeneralise use words like (_some many most_, _usually_, _often_, _frequently_, _may_, _might_, _could_, _tend to_, _be likely to_)

### Come si viene valutati?
Esistono 4 criteri di valutazione (esattamente come per il task 1) ma con qualche differenza nel task achievement:
1. task achievement
   - faccio quello che mi viene chiesto di fare
   - argomentare in modo bilanciato entrambi gli elementi dati
   - tirare fuori la propria opinione specialmente se attivamente richiesta
   - non andare fuori tema
2. struttura
   - testo coeso e scorrevole, no testo schematico. usare i sentence linkers 
   - va benissimo creare frasi corte, con punteggiatura. però inizia le nuove frasi con i sentence linkers
   - importante la divisione in paragrafi (sempre 4), inserire linea vuota tra un paragrafo e l’altro
3. grammatica
   - grammatical accuracy (precisione e correttezza): scrivere semplice semplice
   - grammatical range (importante per C1 e C2)
4. lessico
   - varietà lessicale, non significa parole difficili ma varietà.

### Paragrafo 1: introduction
- 30-60 parole
- The introduction mainly consists of
  - paraphrasing the task sentence, using synonyms or changing the form of the words (e.g. employment, employed), and
  - a summary of your opinion (if required by the task).

Examples of task sentence paraphrases:
- _While studying abroad provides an opportunity to broaden one's experience, it also presents the danger of negative influences from the host culture._
→ _Studying abroad is a potential source of both knowledge about life and the risk of adverse foreign cultural influences._
- _People increasingly access the news online these days rather than buying newspapers and magazines to find out what is going on in the world._
→ _Nowadays, there is a growth in the number of people accessing the news online, avoiding buying newspapers and magazines to know what is happening in the world._
- _Some people say that parents should encourage their children to take part in organised group activities in their free time. Others say that it is important for children to learn how to occupy themselves on their own. Discuss both these views and give your opinion._
→ _There are individuals who believe that parents should motivate their children to participate in organised group activities during their leisure time, while others argue that it is crucial for children to find out ways to entertain themselves._

### Paragrafo 2: main body 1
- 70-100 parole
- Prendiamo idee, argomentazioni ed esempi legati al primo elemento. SOLO del primo elemento, no mischioni
- non mettiamo opinioni personali, non dobbiamo dire “per me i vantaggi sono …”. usare: some, many, most, usually, often, frequently, may, might, could, tend to, be likely to
- però si può dire “un vantaggio potrebbe essere … infatti …”
- posso portare esempi personali purché funzionali ad argomentare qualcosa.

### Paragrafo 3: main body 2
- 70-100 parole
- idem ma con secondo argomento

### Paragrafo 4: conclusion
- 30-60 parole
- opinione nel dettaglio: _I believe_, _I think_, …
- non serve argomentare di nuovo

### Types and strategies
**Advantages & Disadvantages**
eg. _Nowadays, more people move away from their friends and families for work, does this development have more advantages or disadvantages?_
1. intro: parafrasi + summary of your opinion
2. body paragraph 1: describe 2-3 disadvantages
3. body paragraph 2: describe 2-3 advantages
4. conclusion: your opinion (use: _I believe_, _I think_, _in my view_, _in my opinion_)

**Two views + your opinion**
eg. _Some people believe getting a degree is the best way to find a job. Others believe employers value work experience more than a university degree. Discuss both views and give your opinion._
1. intro: parafrasi + summary of your opinion
2. body paragraph 1: describe view 1
3. body paragraph 2: describe view 2
4. conclusion: your opinion (eg. _Although a university degree may be important for recent university graduates, who have very little or no work experience, I believe that, in most cases, the hands-on experience and proven skills of a potential job candidate matter more than his or her education_)

**Opinion essay**
eg. _In some countries many children are overweight as a result of eating too much fast food. Banning fast food from school canteens is the best way to solve the problem. To what extent you agree or disagree? (in che misura dei d’accordo o in disaccordo?)_
- agree/disagree
  1. intro: parafrasi + summary of your opinion
  2. body paragraph 1: 2-3 reason why you agree or disagree
  3. body paragraph 2: refute 2-3 counter arguments
  4. conclusion: your opinion
- balanced answer
  1. intro: parafrasi + summary of your opinion
  2. body paragraph 1: 2-3 reason why you agree
  3. body paragraph 2: : 2-3 reason why you disagree
  4. conclusion: _I agree that …. but I disagree that …._ (eg. _I agree fast food should be banned from school canteens but I do not think this is the only thing that needs to be done to prevent child obesity_)

**Problem & solutions**
here you are not required to express your opinion. 
eg. _People in major cities are exposed to high levels of pollution which causes severe health problems. What are the causes? What can be done to solve such a problem?_
1. intro: parafrasi + summary of your main body
2. body paragraph 1: 2-3 causes
3. body paragraph 2: 2-3 solutions
4. conclusion: your position on how to solve the problem

**Two direct questions**
eg. _Nowadays, people do not exercise enough. What problems can that cause? What can be done to solve it?_
1. intro: parafrasi
2. body paragraph 1: 2-3 consequences of not exercising
3. body paragraph 2: 2-3 ways to make people exercise more
4. conclusion: brief summary of what mentioned in the main body.
