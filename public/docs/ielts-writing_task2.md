## ✍️ IELTS Task 2: Essay Writing

### 1. Opening Phrases
| Function            | Phrases                                                                                             |
| :------------------ | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **General Openers** | *It is widely accepted that...* / *Nowadays...* / *There is no denying that...*                     |
| **Debate**          | *People are divided in their opinion regarding...* / *Whether [A] or [B] is a controversial issue.* |
| **Common Views**    | *It is a common opinion that...* / *It is often said that...*                                       |

### 2. Expressing Opinion
* **Neutral:** *In my opinion, ...* / *I believe that...* / *It seems to me that...*
* **Formal:** *As far as I am concerned, ...* / *From my perspective, ...* / *I hold the opinion that...*
* **Strong:** *I strongly/firmly agree with the idea that...* / *I strongly opine that...*

### 3. Starting Body Paragraphs
* *According to many...*
* *First and foremost, ...*
* *On the one hand...* (usually followed by *On the other hand...*)
* *It is true that...* / *It is worth considering...*

### 4. Comparison Phrases
* **(Far/Slightly) Higher than / Lower than**
* **In complete contrast to...**
* **Compared to...** / **Unlike...**
* **Twice as much / Three times as much**
* **The most obvious difference is...**

---

### 5. Academic Synonyms
| Simple Word   | Better Alternative                       |
| :------------ | :--------------------------------------- |
| **Big**       | Massive, Enormous, Huge                  |
| **Small**     | Minuscule, Tiny, Negligible              |
| **Good**      | Excellent, Positive, Great               |
| **Bad**       | Dreadful, Awful, Horrible, Unpleasant    |
| **Easy**      | Straightforward, Simple, Effortless      |
| **Difficult** | Challenging, Arduous, Demanding          |
| **Problem**   | Issue, Obstacle, Challenge, Complication |
| **Solution**  | Resolution, Answer                       |

---

### 6. Conclusions
* *In conclusion, ...*
* *To sum up, ...*
* *Ultimately, ...*
* *As a final point, ...*
* *In summary, ...*
