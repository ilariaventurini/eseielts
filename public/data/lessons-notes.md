Channel: * English with Lucy * (or similar B2 channels)

Actionable Learning Tips:
Never Translate Directly: Prepositions don't translate word-for-word between languages. Always learn verbs, nouns, and adjectives with their attached prepositions as a single unit (e.g., learn good at instead of just at).
Learn Rules for Place and Time: Use in for large areas/long periods, on for surfaces/specific days, and at for specific points/times.
Keep a Chunk Notebook: Whenever you read or hear an unfamiliar phrase with a preposition, write the entire phrase in a notebook and create your own example sentences to practice.
